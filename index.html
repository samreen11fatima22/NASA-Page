Humanity’s First Lunar Outpost

NASA is actively planning the Moon Base, humanity's first lunar outpost located near the Moon's South Pole. This site is designed for astronauts to live, work, and conduct long-term scientific research, serving as a stepping stone for future crewed missions to Mars.
